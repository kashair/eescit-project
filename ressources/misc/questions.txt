Statements – Political Test

Economic

1.  The state increases taxes to fund more public services.
2.  A universal basic income is introduced for all citizens.
3.  Large corporations are more strictly regulated by the state.
4.  Privatizing public services improves their efficiency.
5.  The minimum wage is increased regularly.
6.  International free trade benefits the national economy.
7.  The state intervenes to rescue struggling companies.
8.  Reducing public spending is an economic priority.
9.  Economic inequalities are acceptable in a high-performing economy.
10. Banks are more strictly controlled by public authorities.

Environmental

1.  Governments strongly limit carbon emissions even if economic growth
    slows.
2.  Nuclear energy is part of the ecological transition.
3.  Combustion-engine vehicles are banned in the long term.
4.  Environmental taxes effectively reduce pollution.
5.  Organic farming receives strong public subsidies.
6.  Polluting companies face higher financial penalties.
7.  Biodiversity protection takes priority over urban development.
8.  Renewable energy can fully replace fossil fuels.
9.  Citizens change their lifestyles to fight climate change.
10. International environmental agreements are sufficiently effective.

Societal

1.  The state guarantees universal access to healthcare.
2.  Immigration is overall beneficial for society.
3.  Affirmative action policies are necessary to reduce inequalities.
4.  Public surveillance is justified to ensure security.
5.  Individual rights take priority over collective security.
6.  The legal retirement age is increased.
7.  The state intervenes more to reduce poverty.
8.  Social media platforms are more strictly regulated by law.
9.  Gender equality has been achieved in modern societies.
10. Social policies prioritize equality rather than individual
    responsibility.

Cultural

1.  The state funds culture and the arts.
2.  Globalization threatens local cultures.
3.  Public media are necessary in a democracy.
4.  Teaching national history is a priority in schools.
5.  Controversial works are censored in public spaces.
6.  The state encourages the protection of regional languages.
7.  Popular culture is as valuable as so-called “classical” culture.
8.  Digital platforms promote local cultural production.
9.  National cultural identity is essential to social cohesion.
10. Cultural subsidies are allocated based on public interest rather
    than artistic freedom.

Industrial

1.  The state financially supports strategic national industries.
2.  Industrial reshoring is a political priority.
3.  Strict environmental standards harm industrial competitiveness.
4.  Industrial automation threatens employment.
5.  Heavy industries are gradually replaced by more sustainable sectors.
6.  International supply chains are too risky.
7.  The state imposes national production quotas.
8.  Public investments in industrial infrastructure are essential.
9.  International competition is limited to protect local industry.
10. Industrial companies play a greater role in vocational training.

Technological

1.  Artificial intelligence is strongly regulated by governments.
2.  Data protection limits technological innovation.
3.  Large technology companies have too much power.
4.  The state invests massively in scientific research.
5.  Surveillance technologies are allowed for national security.
6.  Internet access is considered a fundamental right.
7.  Robots and automation require new social policies.
8.  Cryptocurrencies are regulated like traditional banks.
9.  Digital platforms are responsible for content published by their
    users.
10. Technological development improves overall human quality of life.
