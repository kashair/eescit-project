Statements – Political Test

Economic

1.  The state should increases taxes to fund more public services.
2.  A universal basic income should be introduced for all citizens.
3.  Large corporations should be more strictly regulated by the state.
4.  Privatizing public services improves their efficiency.
5.  The minimum wage should be increased regularly.
6.  International free trade benefits the national economy.
7.  The state should intervene to rescue struggling companies.
8.  Reducing public spending is an economic priority.
9.  Economic inequalities are acceptable in a high-performing economy.
10. Banks are more strictly controlled by public authorities.

Environmental

1.  Governments should strongly limit carbon emissions even if economic growth
    slows.
2.  Nuclear energy is part of the ecological transition.
3.  Combustion-engine vehicles should be banned in the long term.
4.  Environmental taxes effectively reduce pollution.
5.  Organic farming should receive strong public subsidies.
6.  Polluting companies should face higher financial penalties.
7.  Biodiversity protection should take priority over urban development.
8.  Renewable energy should fully replace fossil fuels.
9.  Citizens should change their lifestyles to fight climate change.
10. International environmental agreements should be sufficiently effective.

Societal

1.  The state should guarantee universal access to healthcare.
2.  Immigration is overall beneficial for society.
3.  Affirmative action policies are necessary to reduce inequalities.
4.  Public surveillance is justified to ensure security.
5.  Individual rights take priority over collective security.
6.  The legal retirement age should be increased.
7.  The state should intervene more to reduce poverty.
8.  Social media platforms should be more strictly regulated by law.
9.  Gender equality has been achieved in modern societies.
10. Social policies should prioritize equality rather than individual
    responsibility.

Cultural

1.  The state should fund culture and the arts.
2.  Globalization threatens local cultures.
3.  Public media are necessary in a democracy.
4.  Teaching national history should be a priority in schools.
5.  Controversial works should not be censored in public spaces.
6.  The state should encourage the protection of regional languages.
7.  Popular culture is as valuable as so-called “classical” culture.
8.  Digital platforms promote local cultural production.
9.  National cultural identity is essential to social cohesion.
10. Cultural subsidies are allocated based on public interest rather
    than artistic freedom.

Industrial

1.  The state should financially support strategic national industries.
2.  Industrial reshoring should be a political priority.
3.  Strict environmental standards should harm industrial competitiveness.
4.  Industrial automation should threaten employment.
5.  Heavy industries should be gradually replaced by more sustainable sectors.
6.  International supply chains should be too risky.
7.  The state should impose national production quotas.
8.  Public investments in industrial infrastructure should be essential.
9.  International competition should be limited to protect local industry.
10. Industrial companies should play a greater role in vocational training.

Technological

1.  Artificial intelligence should be strongly regulated by governments.
2.  Data protection should limit technological innovation.
3.  Large technology companies should have too much power.
4.  The state should invest massively in scientific research.
5.  Surveillance technologies should be allowed for national security.
6.  Internet access should be considered a fundamental right.
7.  Robots and automation should require new social policies.
8.  Cryptocurrencies should be regulated like traditional banks.
9.  Digital platforms should be responsible for content published by their
    users.
10. Technological development should improve overall human quality of life.
